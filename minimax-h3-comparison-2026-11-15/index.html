15 November 2026 · RTX 5060 Ti, 16 GB VRAM · 64 GB system memory

Measured on ComfyUI 0.44.0 before the separate H3 Image setup.

Fastest measured warmed run: Eros beta 7 fix1 learned HQ, 80.5 s (1.82× the baseline throughput). This is a speed result; quality and task differences are considered separately below.

Eight completed image-to-video generations: one benign synthetic office scene, two fixed seeds, 512 × 768, 124 frames at 24 fps (5.17 s), with audio. The source, prompt, encoder and VAEs were shared. This is a screening comparison of two samples per configuration, not a universal quality ranking.

What limits the comparison

The baseline and optimized W4A8 use the same beta 5 tune and identical sampling settings; that pair isolates the model representation most directly. Calibrated NVFP4 uses base H3 and 20 steps rather than the Eros tune’s 8. Beta 7 also changes the tune, sampler, scheduler and sparse attention. Its timing difference cannot be attributed to checkpoint precision alone. Native VSA sparse execution was observed in both beta 7 runs. ComfyUI also reported that this checkpoint has no learned coarse gate branch; its VSA fine stage ran without that branch.

Operating-system file caches were not controlled. Model downloads and separate image-model staging overlapped portions of the batch, and disk/network load was not isolated. Small timing differences should be treated cautiously. Sampler times are websocket node wall times, including loading/transfers; they are not GPU kernel timings. GPU memory was sampled every 2 s across the whole board, including desktop use; process RSS covers the server process. Sampled peaks can miss brief spikes. Weight sizes use decimal GB; memory readings use GiB.

Output checks and quality notes

All eight audited files contain the expected 124-frame, 512 × 768, 24 fps video and stereo 32 kHz audio. Decoded AAC samples exceeded full scale in 7 of the eight clips; that metric alone does not establish audible clipping. Frame differences and audio measurements confirm changing imagery and audio samples, not natural motion, intelligibility or exact dialogue.

Current Eros beta 5 INT8

Both clips preserve the subject, glasses, clothing and office, and perform the small arm/head movement. Fruit shape/placement changes more in one seed. No gross visual failure in the sampled frames.

Eros beta 5 optimized W4A8

Both clips preserve the same subject and office and perform the movement. Sampled visual quality is close to current INT8, with small pose/hand/texture differences. These samples do not establish zero quantization loss.

Model source

Base H3 calibrated NVFP4

Both seeds preserve the subject and office and perform the requested small movement. Sampled frames show no clear visual improvement over beta5 W4A8 in this scene. The 20-step base recipe uses less server RAM but takes substantially longer per clip; it changes the tune as well as quantization. Speech accuracy was not transcribed.

Model source

Eros beta 7 fix1 learned HQ

Both seeds preserve the subject, clothing and office and perform the arm/head movement without a gross visual failure in the sampled frames. The hand initially rises before lowering, so action timing differs slightly from the request. This is the fastest tested configuration, with a larger server RAM footprint than beta5 W4A8. Native sparse execution was observed; the runtime reports no learned coarse gate branch. Only the single-pass recipe was tested.
