Build plan after approval

  1. Input and jobs. Local browser UI, audio upload, format conversion, progress, cancellation, and saved local projects.
  2. Transcription. Start with a clean-piano model; add mixed-song separation and an experimental melody-plus-harmony piano-cover path.
  3. Review and practice. Editable notes, left/right hand assignment, falling-note playback, speed and looping; add MIDI keyboard feedback after the base viewer.
  4. Exports and validation. MIDI, MusicXML, printable PDF, plus song-level checks against real authorized recordings.

Design decisions to approve

First release: a local app on this Fedora machine, with no account and no cloud upload of recordings.

Song modes: distinguish faithful piano extraction from a generated piano cover. Show uncertainty and let you correct notes.

Video links: the field is designed for content you may process. The implementation plan needs a permitted way to obtain its audio; a link alone cannot guarantee access.

Quality bar: clean solo piano first. Mixed commercial songs need separation and manual correction, so “any song, exact notes” is not a promise.
