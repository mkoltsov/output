Business expense checklist for self-employed project managers

A consolidated table of common potential deductions, practical examples and the conditions that determine the claim. Equipment, supplies, services and travel generally require an ordinary and necessary business purpose. Personal use is excluded. Dollar amounts are USD.

Allocate actual business use

Keep receipts, payment records and a reasonable allocation. Using something for work does not automatically make its full cost deductible.

$2,500 is a small-purchase threshold

For a business without an applicable financial statement, eligible items up to $2,500 per invoice or separately substantiated item may be expensed under the safe harbour. Bookkeeping and an annual timely election are required. This is not an annual total cap.

Use the correct tax year

Equipment timing generally follows when it is placed in business service. Ordinary expenses follow the accounting method. Financing principal is not a second deduction. Review missed prior-year costs through the applicable correction rules.

Applying the table
  • Each item needs its own business purpose: several computers or chairs can qualify, but quantity alone establishes no deduction. Home-office eligibility is separate from eligibility for business furniture and equipment.
  • Larger assets may qualify for Section 179 or special depreciation, or be depreciated over time. Section 179 has annual dollar, taxable-business-income and business-use conditions. IRS: depreciation ↗
  • Personal groceries, normal clothing, family subscriptions, ordinary commuting, entertainment and loan principal generally do not qualify as business operating expenses. Handle reimbursements consistently; avoid duplicate deductions.
  • Qualifying business deductions generally remain available when the taxpayer takes the standard deduction. A deduction reduces taxable income; it is not a refund of the purchase price.
  • State treatment can differ from federal treatment, including North Carolina equipment adjustments. NCDOR: equipment adjustments ↗
