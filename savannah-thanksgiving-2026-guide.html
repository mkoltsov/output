Suggested sequence · All times Eastern

Five days across the coast.

25Wednesday

Beaufort on the way in; Savannah for the night.

Use the arrival journey to see another destination. For an approach along I-95 from the north, keep a Florence picnic stop to 30 minutes, then aim for Beaufort's waterfront around 14:00–15:00. Allow an hour to stroll Henry C. Chambers Waterfront Park and Bay Street, with a short look along the historic side streets.

Use public marina/waterfront parking and read its payment signs; Beaufort meters normally operate 11:00–19:00 Monday–Saturday. Budget $5–10 for parking as an allowance, not a quoted tariff. The waterfront walk itself needs no attraction ticket.

Continue to Savannah, allowing roughly 1–1¼ hours, plus traffic. Target the Midtown hotel around 16:15–17:15. If the drive runs late, shorten Beaufort to a quick stretch rather than trying to fit a museum in. Check-in begins at 15:00.

River Street is an optional evening stroll if everyone has energy. Eat, then buy picnic supplies for Thursday and the coastal outings.

26Thursday

Thanksgiving among the squares.

09:30–12:30: start at Forsyth Park's north entrance, Bull and Gaston Streets. Walk north through Monterey Square, pause along Jones Street, then continue via Madison, Chippewa, Wright and Johnson squares toward the riverfront. Allow about 3–4 km with short detours, photo stops and benches; this is a planning estimate.

Keep lunch simple with Wednesday's groceries. Take an afternoon hotel break or shorten the walk. Return to the waterfront later only if everyone wants more. Outdoor sightseeing is free; avoid relying on museum access or unconfirmed holiday restaurant hours.

27Friday

Jekyll Island by day; Savannah tree lighting at night.

08:00: leave Midtown. Allow 1¾–2¼ hours each way, including island access and holiday slack. This is a proper day trip, so keep it to three stops. Vehicle entry is normally $10; selected holiday dates add $5. Budget $15; the exact 27 November surcharge was not confirmed.

10:00–11:00: walk the Historic District's grounds and riverfront. This is an outdoor, self-guided visit; hotel interiors and paid tours are not included. 11:15–11:45: see the Horton House ruins, a short free history stop. 12:00–12:30: picnic lunch.

12:45–14:00: Driftwood Beach for the weathered trees, sand and photographs. The nearby St Simons NOAA station predicts high tide at 10:00 and low tide at 16:41 that day. This visit is on the falling tide, not at low tide; actual beach access can differ. If access is poor, spend the time around the historic district instead.

Leave Jekyll at 14:00; 14:30 is the latest planned departure. Aim for the hotel around 16:00–16:45, then change and eat early. Be parked downtown by about 17:30 and at MLK Park around 18:00 for the free 18:30–19:30 tree lighting.

This timetable returns to Savannah before dark. Jekyll's own evening lights are an alternative evening plan, requiring the Savannah tree lighting to be dropped.

28Saturday

Fort Pulaski and Tybee; boats after dark.

08:30: suggested departure from Midtown; allow roughly 45–60 minutes to the fort as a planning allowance. 09:30–11:30: explore Fort Pulaski, the trip's main military-history stop. Use 101 Fort Pulaski Road for navigation. Entry is $10 per person aged 16+, with younger visitors free; the illustrative group allowance is $20. Pay by card at the entrance.

12:00–14:30: picnic and walk at Tybee's North Beach. Optional lighthouse admission adds $52 for the example group; allow 45–75 minutes. Otherwise keep the beach visit inexpensive with two hours of public parking, budget $8.

Leave Tybee around 14:30–15:00, allowing about an hour back toward Midtown plus traffic. Rest and eat early. Return downtown with time to park, and reach Plant Riverside/River Street around 18:15–18:30. The free boat parade starts at 19:00, making two passes along the riverfront between Eastern Wharf and the Talmadge Bridge.

Avoidable expenses and useful backups

Make the holiday schedule work.

Parking: use the holiday exemption.

Savannah officially suspends parking-meter and time-zone enforcement on Thursday 26 and Friday 27 November. Other restrictions still apply, and garages charge separately. A Wednesday purchase of a 48-hour street pass offers little value over that free period.

Normal weekend flat rates at Bryan, Liberty, Robinson and State garages are $5/day; the city also lists $25 special-event rates. The rate applied during the parade is unconfirmed. Read the entrance sign and allow time for a full garage.

Tybee is separate: public parking costs $4/hour, 08:00–20:00 every day, including holidays; use its app or kiosk.

Weather and meal contingencies.

  • Friday and Saturday are the two full coastal outings. In poor weather, replace one with the indoor museum rather than cramming both into one day.
  • Fort Pulaski normally opens 09:00–17:00; the fort closes at 16:30. Both the park and fort close on Thanksgiving.
  • The lighthouse sells tickets onsite only, normally until 16:30 on Friday/Saturday; weather can close the tower. It closes on Thanksgiving.
  • Savannah History Museum, 303 Martin Luther King Jr Boulevard, is the indoor alternative: 09:00–16:00, closed Thanksgiving. Allow 60–90 minutes and $44 for the example group; pay onsite. Choosing it replaces outdoor time.
  • Buy Thursday picnic supplies on Wednesday. A specific Thanksgiving restaurant opening or table has not been verified.
  • Use comfortable shoes for uneven historic streets and layers for the riverfront. Recheck weather and event notices before each outing.
