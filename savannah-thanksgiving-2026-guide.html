What makes each stop worth the time.

Aircraft & immersive history · 2–2½ hours

Mighty Eighth Air Force Museum

See the restored B-17 “City of Savannah,” other aircraft exhibits, mission films and navigator/gunner interactives. Reviews particularly value the historical storytelling and docents. It is a compact WWII museum with a substantial visit, rather than an all-day theme park.

Group admission $48; parking free. General admission does not establish access inside the aircraft.

Tripadvisor: observed 4.8/5Current B-17 exhibit
Island expedition · A full outing

Cumberland Island

The ferry is part of the experience: arrive at an island with Dungeness mansion ruins, maritime forest, an undeveloped shoreline and free-roaming horses. Explore the ruin grounds and exterior, then a manageable forest/beach walk. Horse sightings vary.

Group base $186 ferry + park combined; budget $200–215 with possible checkout charges. Bring all food and water; no ordinary island restaurants.

Working animal hospital · 60–90 minutes

Georgia Sea Turtle Center

Visit a real rehabilitation facility, with patient-viewing areas and interactive exhibits. This is an engaging short visit; do not expect a large aquarium. Patient numbers and treatment activity vary, and admission does not promise feeding or animal handling.

Group base $46 plus any tax/fees; Jekyll entry is separate. Behind-the-scenes upgrades require their own availability check.

Tripadvisor: observed 4.5/5Official experience & admission
Wildlife boat · 75 minutes

Captain Derek’s Dolphin Adventure

A narrated outing through Tybee’s coastal waters, with dolphin watching and lighthouse views. Reviews describe the boat ride and crew as a highlight. Dolphin behaviour depends on the animals and conditions; wake-surfing is not a promised performance.

Group base $127.80 before tax/fees. Budget $145–160. The checked Saturday slot is 14:00–15:15.

A day-by-day plan with real anchors.

25Wednesday

A road stop, then the B-17.

For an I-95 approach from the north, stop at Buc-ee’s Florence, 3390 North Williston Road, around late morning. Allow 30–45 minutes for food and a break; barbecue sandwiches start at 10:30. It operates 24 hours, including holidays.

14:00–16:30: target the Mighty Eighth museum, 175 Bourne Avenue, Pooler, before heading to the hotel. It opens 10:00–17:00 Wednesday and closes Thanksgiving. Allow 2–2½ hours, with parking free.

If traffic prevents arrival by about 14:30, use Friday for the museum and shorten/drop the Jekyll outing. Do not count on Sunday: the museum opens at noon then. After check-in, dinner and picnic shopping are enough; keep River Street optional.

26Thursday

Cumberland: ferry, ruins, forest and shoreline.

08:15: suggested hotel departure. Allow roughly 2–2½ hours to St Marys, plus traffic. Reach the ferry check-in at 113 St Marys Street West by 10:45, 60 minutes before the 11:45 sailing.

11:45–12:30: ferry to the island. NPS lists stops at both Dungeness and Sea Camp; ask the crew to disembark at Dungeness for the ruins. Confirm the return pickup dock/time before setting out.

12:30–14:00: toilets/water at Dungeness Dock, then the short walk to the ruins and a packed picnic. The ruins are less than 0.8 km from the dock. If everyone is on time and comfortable, add the beach via the forest/boardwalk route, starting back by 15:00. This makes roughly 4–5 km walking in total, an estimate; skip the beach for a shorter ruins visit. Be back at Dungeness Dock around 16:00–16:15 for the 16:45 return. Do not try the full 6.9 km Southend Loop: NPS allows 3–5 hours for that loop alone.

17:30: approximate mainland arrival. Allow a dinner stop and return to Savannah roughly 20:00–21:00, depending on restaurant waits. Pack lunch, water and substantial backup food on Wednesday. Cracker Barrel confirms Thanksgiving service chainwide; a Kingsland stop is an option, with branch hours and waits to refresh that day.

This is the premium expedition day. Wildlife sightings vary; keep at least 15 m from horses. If ferry conditions or the cancellation terms are unsuitable, use the indoor Thanksgiving alternative below.

27Friday

Turtle patients and Driftwood Beach.

08:00: leave Midtown; allow 1¾–2¼ hours to Jekyll including access/holiday slack. 10:00–11:30: Georgia Sea Turtle Center, 214 Stable Road. The centre opens 09:00–17:00 Friday and is closed Thanksgiving.

Picnic, then 12:45–14:00 Driftwood Beach. The nearby NOAA St Simons station predicts high tide at 10:00 and low tide at 16:41; this visit is on the falling tide. Actual access can differ. Use the accessible beach for a short wander and photographs; avoid turning it into another lengthy walk.

14:00 departure is ideal; 14:30 is the latest planned return start. Allow time to rest/eat at the hotel, then reach MLK Park at Plant Riverside by about 18:00 for the free tree lighting, 18:30–19:30. Drop the event if road delays make the day tiring.

28Saturday

A battle-scarred fortress, then dolphins.

08:30: depart Midtown. 09:30–11:30: Fort Pulaski, navigate to 101 Fort Pulaski Road. Cross the drawbridge, inspect artillery damage, explore the casemates/prison and upper level. A ranger programme is a bonus if offered; no cannon-firing session is assumed.

12:00–13:00: lunch. Keep the lighthouse climb optional for another version of the day. 13:40: check in with Captain Derek at 3 Old US Highway 80. The verified dolphin departure runs 14:00–15:15.

Return for a hotel break and early dinner. Reach the riverfront around 18:15–18:30 for the free boat parade starting 19:00. Shore viewing is sufficient; a second paid evening cruise adds little to this schedule.

29Sunday

Breakfast, then the return drive.

Use a 10:00 hotel departure for the northbound return. Santee State Park, 251 State Park Road, can provide a 30–45 minute lakeside picnic/stretch; example group admission is $8. Shorten or skip it if the navigation arrival time deteriorates.

The I-95 northbound South Carolina rest area at road marker 99 is closed; choose another toilet stop. Keep the final day free of a timed attraction.

Practical details that matter.

  • Holiday parking: Savannah meters/time-zone enforcement is suspended Thursday 26 and Friday 27; garages charge separately and other restrictions still apply. Tybee charges $4/hour 08:00–20:00 daily, including holidays.
  • Food: buy Thursday picnic supplies on Wednesday. Cumberland has no ordinary food shops. Budget ample water and carry a dinner backup.
  • Ferry conditions: customer cancellation inside ten days can be nonrefundable; captain-cancelled sailings receive refunds under the operator’s terms. Review the forecast and terms before purchasing.
  • Photographs: destination images illustrate real places, not guaranteed animal sightings, current patient numbers or the state of a future event.
