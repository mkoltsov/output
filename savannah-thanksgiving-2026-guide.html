What changed, and why

A shorter trip needs one expedition, not three.

Keep Cumberland on Thursday.

It is the distinctive nature-and-ruins outing in the original plan, and Thursday is the warmer coastal day. The forecast is breezy, not evidence that the ferry will be cancelled. Keeping one verified ferry excursion preserves the trip’s character.

The price is a long day: approximately 320 km of driving, 90 minutes aboard ferries and about 4 km on foot. If that trade-off is unattractive, use the complete Savannah replacement below before buying ferry tickets.

Use Friday close to Savannah.

The railway ride and STEAM exhibits work in the morning; Fort Pulaski fits the warmer afternoon. Stay downtown for a warm break and early dinner before the free tree lighting. That avoids another roughly 49 km hotel round trip.

Drop Jekyll and the separate dolphin cruise from the primary plan. This removes a second long southern excursion and an exposed boat on Friday’s colder, windier coast. Saturday’s boat parade is also out: the return drive starts that morning.

The recommendation is a complete plan, not a sailing guarantee. Both Thursday ferry legs were checked for the whole example party. No tickets are booked or held. Before paying, accept the operator’s short-notice cancellation rules and the full-day commitment. A dated inventory check establishes seats for sale, not a captain’s future sailing decision.
